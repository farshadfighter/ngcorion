# Device backups overview

Is every device's configuration saved, and what was restored recently? This page shows backup coverage, devices with no backup or an old one, and recent restores. Supported devices: Cisco IOS, FortiGate, Linux, Apache and MongoDB.

## Sections

| Section | What it says |
|---|---|
| Backup coverage | How many supported devices have a recent backup |
| Needs a backup | Devices "Never backed up" or with a backup older than allowed |
| Backups per day | Manual, before hardening and before restore, over 30 days |
| Recent restores | Who, which backup, and the result |

## What to do from here

1. In "Needs a backup", "Back up now" for a single device.
2. If the list is long, put the devices in the group of a [backup schedule](/backup/schedules); one-off backups are not a lasting answer.
3. Look into "Reverted" or "Failed" restores in [Restore history](/backup/restores).
