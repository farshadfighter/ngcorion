# Audit overview

A summary of compliance from each asset's latest audit: compliance score, audited assets, failed controls, critical findings, audit coverage and the compliance trend.

## Sections

| Section | What it says |
|---|---|
| Executive Summary | Compliance score, audited assets, failed controls, critical findings and assets out of compliance |
| Audit Coverage | How many assets are audited and how many are not |
| Findings By Severity | Failed controls per severity |
| Top Failed Controls | Controls that fail on the most assets |
| Compliance Trend | The score over time |
| Remediation Progress | Open findings, fixed this month and the share resolved |
| Per-Asset Compliance | Score and date of each asset's latest audit |

## What to do from here

1. **Coverage:** cover the "not audited" assets with a [group audit](/audit/group-runs) or a [schedule](/audit/schedule-auditing).
2. **Top failed controls:** one fix across every asset has the biggest effect; plan hardening from here.
3. **Trend:** a sudden drop means a configuration changed somewhere; the "Compliance dropped" alert reports the same.
4. "Report" builds the compliance report for management.

## Take care

> Scores are the scores "under the policy": controls the [benchmark policy](/remediation/policy) makes optional or excludes do not count. Users other than system administrators see only their own audits.
