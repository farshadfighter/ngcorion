# Assets overview

The big picture of the inventory: how many assets you have, how many are active, how many were added in the last 30 days, and their mix by type, vendor and operating system. To work on the assets themselves, go to the [Asset list](/assets/inventory).

## Cards

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total assets | Every registered asset |
| Active / inactive assets | By the status set on the asset |
| New assets (30 days) | Assets added in the past month |
| Critical assets | Assets marked "Critical" in the asset form |

## What to read from this page

- **"Unknown" in the charts:** assets whose type, vendor or OS is empty. The gap weakens dynamic groups, end-of-life tracking and CVE matching; fill them in.
- **Many new assets** nobody expected: review auto discovery or the Excel imports.
